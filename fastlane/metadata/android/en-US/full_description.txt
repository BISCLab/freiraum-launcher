<p>Freiraum is a calm home screen that you arrange exactly the way you want. It has no internet permission and collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone.</p>

<p><b>Pages</b></p>
<ul>
<li>A home page plus as many pages as you like, each with its own background, optionally as an endless carousel</li>
<li>Pages are built from rows: element boxes and widgets on a tidy row grid</li>
<li>A Dock at the bottom and a pull-down Panel at the top, shown on every page</li>
<li>Function pages: App list, App grid, Text search and Categories</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Elements</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Everything you can tap is an element: apps, folders, places, links, contacts, shortcuts, action buttons and categories. Set it up once, then place it in the Dock, the Panel, element boxes or folders</li>
<li>"Add to home screen" from other apps, for example a website from the browser</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Categories</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Sort apps and other elements; one app can be in several</li>
<li>Ready-made from what Android knows about an app: games, music, photos and more</li>
<li>Smart categories that fill themselves: newly installed, not used in a while, used often, without category, or your own rule</li>
<li>Their own page with cards, tabs or a list, filters in the App list, results in the text search, and a widget</li>
<li>Folders can follow a category and fill themselves</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Widgets</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Clock, notes, music, weather, categories and empty space, plus regular Android widgets</li>
<li>Music widget limited to the players you choose</li>
<li>Weather comes from a weather app that provides it (Breezy Weather via Gadgetbridge); Freiraum itself never goes online</li>
</ul>

<p><b>App list and search</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Alphabetical App list with a fast A–Z rail, and an App grid with its own search field</li>
<li>Text search across apps, favorites, folders, contacts, system and launcher settings, shortcuts, links, places and categories</li>
<li>Calculator, unit converter, and commands that hand a search to your web, maps or AI app</li>
<li>Rename apps, hide them from the list or search, or reveal them only with a search code</li>
<li>Work profile and private space apps in their own section</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Look and feel</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Light and dark mode, accent color, corner shapes, icon packs and themed icons (Android 13+)</li>
<li>Fill, border and text color for every part, with live previews</li>
<li>Designs (Neon, 3D) and wallpaper packs to download, with pictures for every page and the lock screen</li>
<li>Keyboard bezel over the keyboard and a charging animation</li>
<li>Seven gestures: swipe up and down, double tap, long press, two fingers up and down, pinch</li>
<li>Tablets: held sideways, pages show their rows in two columns</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Accessibility and privacy</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Accessibility mode: solid elements with strong borders, larger icons and text, no motion effects</li>
<li>Settings lock with your screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password)</li>
<li>Asks the keyboard not to remember what you type into searches</li>
<li>Export and import everything as one backup file</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Permissions</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Contacts (optional): to find and place contacts</li>
<li>Notification access (optional): for the music widget and notification dots; only which apps have notifications is seen, never their content</li>
<li>Expand status bar: for gestures that open the notifications or quick settings</li>
<li>Set wallpaper: to put a wallpaper's picture on the lock screen, only when you ask for it</li>
<li>Request delete packages: to uninstall an app from its menu; Android asks you to confirm</li>
<li>Access hidden profiles: to show the apps of your private space (Android 15+)</li>
<li>Use biometric: to unlock the settings with your fingerprint or face</li>
</ul>
